Why patients stop

Four problems.
One identical symptom.

A patient who cannot afford the treatment, cannot use the device properly, stopped on a side effect, or could not find it in stock all look the same in your data. Opposite fixes, and today no way to tell them apart.

Cost

The daily controller costs more and relieves nothing today, so it waits. In Kenya, 6 in 10 asthma patients have no reimbursement at all.

Technique

The dose is taken but never reaches the lungs. Invisible to every prescription record.

Side effects

Stopped quietly between visits, surfaced months later if at all.

Stock-out

Not on the shelf that day. Recorded nowhere.

Prescription data tells you what was written down, not what happened. In a study across 19 sites in Kenya, 4 in 10 asthma patients were buying extra reliever over the counter, almost all of them on top of a prescription they already held. That consumption is invisible to the prescriber and to the sponsor. Play4Health captures the action itself, confirmed by the patient, so a stall is identifiable while the patient is still reachable.

SABINA III Kenya cohort, 405 patients, data collected 2019 [2]. Cited to describe the pattern. Not a Play4Health result.

Why early support pays

Digital support doubles the odds.
Nobody verified it.

9 randomised trials, more than twice the odds of adherence. Every one of them measured it by asking the patient. A patient who stops at month 3 of a 12-month therapy takes 9 months of treatment revenue with them, on top of acquisition spend already made. Play4Health supplies the missing half: photo-confirmed, timestamped, from day 1 of initiation.

OR 2.12
Odds of adherence with app-delivered digital support versus comparator. 9 randomised trials, 988 participants, low heterogeneity. Outcome self-reported.
Armitage, Kassavou & Sutton, BMJ Open 2020;10(1):e032045. Read the study

Trial populations covered cardiovascular disease, depression, Parkinson's disease, psoriasis and multimorbidity, at moderate evidence quality. Not a forecast for any specific programme, and not a Play4Health result.

How it works

One photo.
Four verified outcomes.

Patients participate directly. No app, no hardware, no clinic visit.

Dose or device return confirmed from a link. No login friction.

02

Timeframe rules, duplicate controls and photo context checked instantly.

03

Support at the right moment

How to use the device, what to do when symptoms start, and progress signals that sustain the routine. Air quality alerts on high-pollution days, so patients can protect themselves before symptoms start. No cash incentive tied to any prescription decision.
