Why patients stop

4 reasons patients stop.
Your data sees 1.

Adam is 8. After 3 months without an attack, his mother stops his preventer. Nothing happens. Until 2 a.m., one night in August.

She did nothing wrong. Nobody told her the treatment works precisely because nothing happens.

Adam is a composite, not a real patient. The pattern is the same with a blood pressure pill, a diabetes pen or an epilepsy treatment.

Cost, how it is taken, a side effect, a stock-out. In your data they all look the same.

Cost

Chronic treatment is paid month after month, often out of pocket. When money is short, the next pack waits.

How it is taken

Wrong time, wrong dose, wrong technique. Invisible to every prescription record.

Side effects

Stopped quietly between visits, surfaced months later if at all.

Stock-out

Not on the shelf that day. Recorded nowhere.

Prescription data tells you what was written down, not what happened. Play4Health captures the action itself, so a stall is visible while the patient is still reachable.

Option · Take-back

Adherence tools stop at the dose. We recover what is left.

For tablets, the empty pack is checked at refill. Inhalers and pens are returned at a partner pharmacy, photo-verified, and kept out of the waste stream. Same patient record as the doses.

Compliance

Built for regulated programmes.
From the first patient.

Your compliance, medical and privacy teams will ask. The answers are built in, not added later.

Safety routed
