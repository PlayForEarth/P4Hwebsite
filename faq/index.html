The Platform
Play4Health is a verified patient support platform for markets where patients pay out of pocket. Patients confirm each dose with a photo from their own phone, say why when a dose is missed, and report how they are doing. Programme teams see who continues, who has gone quiet, and why. No app to download, no sensors, no new infrastructure.
Dose confirmations (a time-stamped photo per dose time), treatment continuity over time, reasons for missed doses, health events and side effects reported from home, and, as an option, device returns at the pharmacy (inhalers, pens).
No. Play4Health is a compliance and engagement platform, not a clinical decision support tool and not a medical device. It does not provide treatment recommendations or diagnostic outputs. All clinical decisions remain with the patient's healthcare provider. The platform generates verified data on treatment continuity for patient support, access and real-world evidence programmes.
No app to download. Patients use a web link: nothing to install, and it works offline. They sign in once on their phone. No sensors or hardware. The platform runs through smartphones patients and caregivers already have. This is a core design principle: zero new infrastructure.
Who It's For
Pharmaceutical companies running patient support or access programmes, and the funders, foundations and public health programmes who need to know that patients stay on treatment: for donor reporting, health ministry engagement, or impact measurement.
We start with asthma, diabetes and cardiometabolic conditions, sickle cell disease and epilepsy. The platform is condition-agnostic: any long-term treatment with a near-daily action a patient can confirm.
We are headquartered in Nairobi and start in Kenya, then Uganda. The verification engine behind Play4Health has already captured 20,000+ field actions with Play for Earth. No patient cohort has been deployed yet. The platform is built for low-connectivity settings: it works offline on the phones patients already have.
Yes. The platform suits any organisation running distributed patient or community health programmes that require verified behavioral data: NGOs, public health ministries, development funders, and foundations included. Contact us to discuss programme-specific pricing.

EPR, ESG & Compliance
Many markets are introducing EPR obligations for pharmaceutical packaging, including inhalers and single-use injection devices. Device take-back is an option. When it is switched on, Play4Health generates photo-verified, time-stamped return records, usable as evidence for EPR reporting. Collection and reprocessing stay with your logistics and waste partners.
Yes. Device return volumes and packaging recovery data generated through Play4Health can be structured to support CSRD disclosure and Scope 3 Category 12 (end-of-life treatment of sold products) reporting. We recommend using this data as field-level evidence alongside your broader ESG reporting framework.
Consent comes first, and patients can export or delete their data at any time. Each programme's data is isolated, sponsor views are aggregated with small groups hidden, and every access is logged. Dose photos are deleted after a set retention period. Designed around the Kenya and Uganda data protection acts and GDPR principles.
